Nice to Meet You!

Prazer em conhecer você!

Lesson Objective
Fazer apresentações, cumprimentar pessoas, falar sobre origem e nacionalidade.

A1 · Real Life · Real Conversations

By the end of this unit...

Introduce yourself and other people.
Use common greetings in different situations.
Ask and answer personal information questions.
Talk about countries and nationalities.
Use the verb to be in introductions.
Speak confidently in first conversations.

💬 Conversation

Lucas and Emma meet for the first time. Listen to the conversation and then practice with a partner.

Lucas Hi! My name is Lucas. What’s your name?
Emma Hello, Lucas! My name is Emma. Nice to meet you.
Lucas Nice to meet you too, Emma. Where are you from?
Emma I’m from Canada. I’m Canadian. What about you?
Lucas I’m from Brazil. I’m Brazilian.
Emma That’s interesting! Are you a student?
Lucas Yes, I am. I’m an English student. And you?
Emma Yes, I am. I study English too.
Lucas Great! How are you today?
Emma I’m very good, thank you. How about you?
Lucas I’m great! It’s nice talking to you.
Emma Yes, it is. See you later!
Lucas See you!

1. The sound of TH

The sound of th does not exist in Portuguese.

thank THENK
three THRII
think THINK

2. Long vowel sounds

Some English vowels are longer than Portuguese vowels.

📖 Reading

My First Conversation

Lucas is a new student at an international school. On his first day, he meets Emma.

A Hello! My name is Lucas. What’s your name?
B Hi! I’m Emma. Nice to meet you.
A Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?
B I’m from Canada. I’m Canadian. What about you?
A I’m from Brazil. I’m Brazilian.
B That’s great! Are you a student?
A Yes, I am. I’m an English student.
B How are you today?
A I’m very good, thank you.
B Great! Welcome to our school.

Reading Comprehension

1. What’s the boy’s name?

2. Where is Emma from?

3. What is Lucas’ nationality?

4. Are Lucas and Emma students?

5. How is Lucas today?
